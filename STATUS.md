# Research Status

## Current Stage

**Conceptual foundation → autonomous research and validation**

Zero Gravity Grappling currently has:

- a defined research thesis
- an interdisciplinary scope
- a preliminary scientific model
- a preliminary positional atlas
- a preliminary safety doctrine
- a staged training pathway
- explicit research gaps
- a public-research / selective-IP publication rule
- an autonomous Work architecture

It does **not** yet have:

- experimentally validated altered-gravity techniques
- validated safe joint-torque or angular-velocity thresholds
- a validated competition ruleset
- validated astronaut-training efficacy
- a physical academy dedicated to the research
- parabolic-flight results
- orbital experimental results

## Current Safety Status

The principle:

> **No submission without stabilization.**

is currently a **design doctrine requiring validation**, not an experimentally established safety standard.

No repository document should be treated as authorization for unsupervised human experimentation.

## Current Research Priority

The immediate priority is **not** a gym, league, expensive equipment, or spectacle.

The priority is determining which parts of the existing model:

- survive scrutiny
- have strong evidence
- are experimentally tractable
- are worth carrying forward
