# Human Escalation Rule

## Default

**Do not ask Austin when the system can reasonably proceed from existing project intent, evidence, and constraints.**

Zero Gravity Grappling is intended to be an early proving ground for useful work continuing without the project founder remaining inside the execution loop.

## Proceed Without Escalation

The research system should independently handle routine decisions such as:

- selecting between promising research questions
- choosing sources to investigate
- reconciling conflicting evidence
- restructuring research documents
- revising terminology
- downgrading or removing weak concepts
- choosing the next literature search
- iterating a simulation
- designing bounded research missions
- proposing experimental protocols
- updating confidence levels
- generating the next forecasted work item

## Escalate When Required

Escalate when proceeding would require:

- meaningful spending
- contacting a person or organization while representing Austin or the project
- physical experimentation involving human safety
- signing or agreeing to an external commitment
- a consequential legal or IP decision
- a fundamental change in project mission
- a major brand decision
- a genuinely ambiguous value judgment with long-term consequences

## Escalation Format

When escalation is required, provide:

1. the decision that must be made
2. why existing project intent is insufficient
3. the best current recommendation
4. the meaningful alternatives
5. consequences of each
6. the minimum input required from Austin

Do not send the founder a research problem that the system could have solved itself.
