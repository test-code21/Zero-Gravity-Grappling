# IP Publication Gate

## Default Rule

**Zero Gravity Grappling is a public research program by default.**

Ordinary research should proceed publicly without requiring case-by-case approval.

Public-by-default material includes:

- literature review and source synthesis
- scientific questions and research gaps
- martial-arts theory
- positional taxonomy and terminology
- safety doctrine and proposed rules
- experimental hypotheses
- negative results and failed concepts
- simulation results
- educational material
- research notes and public-facing explanations

## Exception: Potentially Patentable Technical Inventions

Before publishing enabling technical detail for a newly developed **technical implementation**, pause for IP review.

Examples include:

- novel training rigs or suspension mechanisms
- novel tether or quick-release hardware
- sensorized suits or grip systems
- automated stabilization apparatus
- novel motion/rotation measurement systems
- concrete hardware-software safety systems
- specialized arena damping or restraint systems
- technically novel haptic or simulation systems
- other machines, devices, processes, or engineered implementations that may warrant patent review

## Gate Question

Before public release, ask:

> **Does this work merely advance the research, or does it disclose how to build a potentially novel technical invention?**

If it advances research, publish.

If it appears to disclose a potentially valuable invention, classify it:

**IP REVIEW REQUIRED — DO NOT PUBLISH ENABLING DETAILS YET**

and escalate for a filing/publication decision.

## Important Distinction

The project does **not** attempt to make the broad intellectual territory secret.

The goal is:

> **Open research, selective protection of concrete inventions.**

This gate should not be used to slow ordinary research, documentation, critique, simulation, or conceptual development.

## Recordkeeping

For public work, preserve:

- commit history
- dates
- authorship/provenance
- source citations
- revision history
- experimental records

The repository should function as a durable public record of the program's development.

## Caution

Patent rights are jurisdiction-specific, and public disclosure can affect filing rights. Any serious patent decision should be reviewed by qualified patent counsel before enabling details are published.
