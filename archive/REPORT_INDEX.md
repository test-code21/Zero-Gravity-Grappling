## Why They Are Not Yet Canonical Doctrine

The historical reports contain valuable synthesis, but some citations were recorded as internal research-environment markers rather than durable public references.

They also mix:

- established science
- martial-arts inference
- design proposals
- speculative concepts

Before promoting claims from these reports into current truth, the program should:

1. normalize important citations
2. verify source-to-claim support
3. classify claims under `research/EVIDENCE_STANDARD.md`
4. preserve unresolved contradictions
5. update current truth only where warranted

## Preservation Rule

When the original reports are imported into this directory, preserve them unchanged as historical artifacts.

Corrections belong in later review documents and current-truth updates, not silent edits to the archive.

## Work Consequence

Citation normalization and claim extraction should occur only when it has enough information value to justify bounded Work.

The archive exists to preserve reasoning history; it should not become a second competing source of current truth.
