# Research Archive

This directory is reserved for long-form research reports, historical synthesis, superseded-but-useful work, and source-heavy exploratory documents.

## Archive Rule

Archive material is **evidence and history**, not automatically current doctrine.

When an archived report conflicts with `research/CURRENT_TRUTH.md`, the current-truth layer governs until new evidence justifies revision.

## Initial Source Reports

The project currently has several long-form deep-research reports that informed:

- the scientific foundation
- the positional atlas
- safety and stabilization thinking
- training pathways
- simulation/visualization planning
- business and cultural possibilities

Those reports should be normalized and imported here as a later bounded Work item rather than copied blindly into doctrine.

Preserve citations and provenance during import.
