**GitHub issue:** https://github.com/test-code21/Zero-Gravity-Grappling/issues/1

### Objective

Determine what Zero Gravity Grappling should learn next.

### Inputs

The complete current research corpus, including:

- project brief
- core thesis
- scientific foundations
- martial arts foundations
- positional atlas
- safety framework
- training pathway
- research gaps
- visualization strategy
- deep research reports
- current governance rules
- evidence and provenance standards

### Instruction

Analyze the full Zero Gravity Grappling corpus.

Identify unresolved questions whose answers would most materially change:

- the scientific model
- positional system
- safety framework
- experimental pathway
- practical usefulness
- long-term viability of the research program

Rank candidate questions using:

1. expected information gain
2. downstream importance
3. current uncertainty
4. availability and quality of evidence
5. ability to investigate with present tools
6. cost of being wrong

Select the highest-value frontier and instantiate bounded research missions to investigate it.

Do **not** expand the concept merely because expansion is possible.

Prefer:

- resolving uncertainty
- falsifying weak assumptions
- improving evidence quality
- strengthening useful models
- identifying what should be abandoned

Do not ask Austin to choose among routine research directions.

Escalate only under the Human Escalation Rule.

This Work item is complete when:

1. the research frontier has been ranked,
2. the first bounded research mission has been selected,
3. the rationale is documented,
