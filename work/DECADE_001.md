# Decade 001 — Autonomous Research

## Meaning of a Decade

A **Decade** is the next ten sequential states of useful work currently believed to best move the project forward.

It is **not** a ten-year plan.

Only the front edge should be admitted as real Work. Later items are forecasts and should be revised when new evidence changes current truth.

## Decade 001 Objective

Demonstrate that Zero Gravity Grappling can continue accumulating **quality-adjusted useful work** after the founder has supplied intent, values, and research direction but is no longer supervising routine execution.

## Forecasted Work States

### 1. Research Director Pass

Read the entire current research corpus.

Identify the highest-leverage unresolved questions.

Rank them by:

- expected information gain
- downstream importance
- current uncertainty
- evidence availability and quality
- ability to investigate with present tools
- cost of being wrong

Select and instantiate the first bounded research missions.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 2. Research Mission 001

Investigate the highest-value unresolved question using primary, technical, and scholarly sources where available.

Explicitly distinguish:

- established science
- interdisciplinary inference
- proposed design
- speculation

Produce a bounded research result that can alter project doctrine.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 3. Doctrine Update 001

Integrate Mission 001 findings into the project's current truth.

Update, as warranted:

- scientific foundations
- terminology
- research gaps
- safety assumptions
- positional confidence
- experimental priorities

Do not preserve a concept merely because it was previously documented.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 4. Research Mission 002

Re-rank the research frontier based on the new current truth.

Select and execute the next high-value mission.

The second mission should be chosen because Mission 001 changed what is worth knowing next, not because it was pre-scripted.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 5. Positional Atlas Adversarial Audit

Attempt to break the existing atlas.

For every foundational position, ask:

- Is the position physically coherent?
- Is it meaningfully distinct from adjacent positions?
- Does it depend on terrestrial assumptions that disappear in altered gravity?
- What evidence supports it?
- What would falsify it?
- Is it sport-relevant, rescue-relevant, training-only, metaphorical, or unsafe?

Merge, rename, downgrade, or remove concepts when appropriate.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 6. Computational Prototype 001

Build the smallest useful computational artifact capable of testing consequential assumptions.

Possible domains include:

- two-body center-of-mass behavior
- angular momentum
- action/reaction under coupled contact
- wall interaction
- tether constraints
- relative orientation
- controlled versus uncontrolled rotation

The prototype should answer research questions, not exist merely as a visual demo.

**Founder involvement:** none expected unless infrastructure or spending is required.

---

### 7. Virtual Experiment Series 001

Use the computational model to run structured tests.

Record:

- hypothesis
- configuration
- assumptions
- result
- failure mode
- uncertainty
- implications for doctrine
- limitations of simulation

The output should identify what simulation can answer and what must eventually be tested physically.

**Founder involvement:** none expected.

---

### 8. Physical Experiment Program 001

Translate accumulated findings into the **cheapest safe physical experiments worth performing**.

Design protocols without assuming immediate execution.

Each proposed experiment should specify:

- research question
- hypothesis
- environment
- participants/skills required
- apparatus
- measurements
- safety constraints
- stopping criteria
- evidence produced
- what decision the result would unlock

**Founder involvement:** required only before actual human experimentation or meaningful expenditure.

---

### 9. Integrated Research Release 001

Publish a coherent release of the research program incorporating the decade's work.

Possible components:

- updated current truth
- revised positional atlas
- revised safety framework
- research mission reports
- simulation/prototype documentation
- experimental program
- unresolved questions
- confidence map
- negative findings

The release should make the project more useful to the next agent, researcher, practitioner, or expert.

**Founder involvement:** optional review only unless an escalation condition is triggered.

---

### 10. Decade 001 Capstone + Decade 002 Proposal

Synthesize what the decade actually changed.

Evaluate:

- useful knowledge gained
- uncertainties reduced
- assumptions falsified
- artifacts created
- evidence quality
- doctrine improvements
- work that failed or produced little value
- agent-hours consumed
- unnecessary human judgment consumed
- remaining frontier

Update the program's current truth.

Then propose **Decade 002**: the next ten forecasted work states that best follow from the evidence now available.

The capstone is both a retrospective and a handoff into the next cycle.

**Founder involvement:** one compact judgment gate only if the proposed next decade contains consequential choices that cannot be inferred from established intent.

## Quality Test

Decade 001 succeeds if the research program is materially better **and** the founder did not have to supervise routine execution.

Progress is not measured by document count or agent-hours alone.

The relevant measure is:

> **quality-adjusted useful work produced per unit of human judgment required.**
