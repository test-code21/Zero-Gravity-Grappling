# Research Frontier

**Status:** awaiting completion of Work 001 — Autonomous Research Director Pass.

This file is the durable handoff between current truth and research execution.

## Frontier Ranking

Work 001 should populate a ranked set of consequential unresolved questions using:

1. expected information gain
2. downstream importance
3. current uncertainty
4. evidence availability and quality
5. executability with present tools
6. cost of being wrong

## Required Output Format

| Rank | Research Frontier | Why It Matters | Current Confidence | Evidence Available | Cheapest Useful Next Step | Work Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Selection Rule

The highest-ranked question does not automatically win.

The Research Director should select the mission with the best **quality-adjusted expected value**, considering:

- whether another mission is a prerequisite
- whether the question can actually be resolved now
- whether the evidence base is mature enough
- whether the project is at risk of polishing a weak premise
- whether a small test could outperform a large literature review

## Anti-Expansion Rule

Do not add research frontiers merely because they are interesting.

A frontier belongs here when resolving it could materially change the project.
