# Terms and Lines

## Preferred Names

- Zero Gravity Grappling
- Altered-Gravity Grappling
- Three-Dimensional Grappling
- Positional Atlas of Zero Gravity Grappling
- Zero Gravity Jiu Jitsu, as a sub-discipline or lineage

## Core Lines

> Base becomes orientation.

> Pressure becomes vector.

> Guard becomes relational control.

> The mat becomes the room.

> The opponent is not beneath you or above you — they are part of your moving reference frame.

> Zero Gravity Grappling is the study of leverage after the floor disappears.

> Martial arts began with gravity. The next frontier is learning what remains when gravity changes.

> The first space sport may not be about speed. It may be about control.

> The first space sport may not begin as spectacle. It may begin as humans learning how to touch, stabilize, and trust each other in a new environment.

> A position can be a controlled motion, not only a static hold.

> The dominant athlete makes the opponent live inside their geometry.

## Avoid

- “MMA in space”
- “floating BJJ” as the whole concept
- assuming all future space life is zero gravity
- ignoring safety, cost, physics, or physiology
- making it sound like a near-term guaranteed startup
- over-mythologizing before the science is clear
