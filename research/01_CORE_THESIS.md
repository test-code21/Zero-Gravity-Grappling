# Core Thesis

## Primary Thesis

**Zero Gravity Grappling is the codification of controllable human relationships in altered gravity.**

It studies how bodies establish:

- center
- orientation
- contact
- leverage
- restraint
- rescue
- safety
- composure
- sport
- play

when the floor no longer functions as the primary reference point.

## Deeper Human Thesis

This is not just “fighting in space.”

It is a way to ask:

> What does the human body become when it is no longer bound to the floor?

And:

> How do humans adapt culturally, athletically, and communally when the environment changes?

## Working Philosophical Frame

Martial arts are embodied philosophies of relationship:

- body to ground
- body to opponent
- body to breath
- body to fear
- body to pressure
- body to uncertainty
- body to space

In altered gravity, those relationships change.

## Key Transformation

Traditional grappling asks:

> How do I control a body against the ground?

Zero Gravity Grappling asks:

> How do I control a body when the ground is no longer the final authority?
