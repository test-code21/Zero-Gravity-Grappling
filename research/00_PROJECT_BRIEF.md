# Project Brief: Zero Gravity Grappling

## One-Sentence Description

**Zero Gravity Grappling is a future-facing sport and human-movement research discipline focused on body-to-body control in altered-gravity environments.**

## Expanded Description

Drawing from jiu-jitsu, wrestling, judo, biomechanics, vestibular science, aerospace medicine, neutral-buoyancy training, and motor learning, Zero Gravity Grappling studies how humans establish orientation, leverage, restraint, rescue, and composure when the floor is no longer the primary reference point.

## Why This Matters

Every sport encodes an environment.

- Surfing answers the ocean.
- Climbing answers the wall.
- Skiing answers snow and slope.
- Jiu-jitsu answers ground pressure.
- Zero Gravity Grappling answers altered gravity, disorientation, and three-dimensional body control.
