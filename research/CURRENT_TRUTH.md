# Current Truth — v0.1

## Program Identity

**Zero Gravity Grappling** is the umbrella research program for altered-gravity human movement, grappling, safety, orientation, restraint, rescue, sport design, and embodied adaptation.

A future terrestrial martial-arts school may exist under a different name. Such a school would be an institution that can host or contribute to the research program; it does not define the program.

## Core Thesis

> Zero Gravity Grappling is the codification of controllable human relationships in altered gravity.

The project studies how bodies establish:

- center
- orientation
- contact
- leverage
- restraint
- rescue
- safety
- composure
- sport
- play

when the floor is no longer the primary reference frame.

## Current Scientific Model

Working propositions include:

- In terrestrial grappling, the floor acts as a crucial external reaction surface.
- In free-space contact, two grapplers form a coupled multi-body system governed by conservation of momentum and angular momentum.
- Traditional top/bottom concepts lose meaning when gravity no longer defines a privileged direction.
- Control may be better described through relative orientation, rotation, contact geometry, external anchors, and escape pathways.
- Walls, tethers, suit grips, and other fixed structures can become substitutes for terrestrial base.
- Human vestibular and sensorimotor adaptation is a central research problem, not a side issue.

These propositions vary in evidentiary maturity and must not all be treated as experimentally validated.

The research program draws from more than Brazilian jiu-jitsu.

Relevant lineages and analogs include:

- jiu-jitsu / jūjutsu
- judo
- wrestling
- sambo
- catch wrestling
- redirection arts
- contact improvisation
- partner acrobatics
- aerial arts
- underwater rescue
- EVA movement
- confined-space stabilization

Important transformed concepts include:

- **Kuzushi:** disruption of usable orientation and recovery pathways
- **Maai:** three-dimensional relational distance and timing
- **Jūshin:** center-of-mass and shared center-of-mass management
- **Tai sabaki:** three-dimensional body reorientation
- **Ukemi:** receiving motion, drift, impact, and rotational force

## Safety Principle

> **No submission without stabilization.**

The current design logic is that joint locks, chokes, and spinal/neck manipulation become especially dangerous when bodies retain uncontrolled relative or shared rotation.

Stabilization must therefore precede any submission threat.

This is a working safety doctrine requiring empirical validation.

## Development Path

The program currently favors a staged progression:

1. theory and terrestrial grappling analysis
2. constrained-floor and wall analogs
3. suspension / harness analogs
4. underwater / neutral-buoyancy analogs
5. VR and physics simulation
6. parabolic-flight microgravity drills
7. eventual orbital research

The order can change when evidence warrants it.

## Public Research Rule

Research is public by default.

Potentially patentable concrete technical inventions pass an IP Publication Gate before enabling details are published.

## Work Rule

The founder has already supplied substantial intent, values, areas of curiosity, and standards of seriousness.

The program should now translate those into bounded Work and continue progressing without routine founder oversight.

Human involvement is reserved for consequential judgment, external commitments, spending, human-safety experiments, and major legal/IP decisions.

## Current Priority

The bottleneck is **not** finding a gym, hiring a trainer, launching a league, or acquiring expensive altered-gravity infrastructure.

The bottleneck is determining which parts of the current model are true, useful, testable, and worth carrying forward.

The immediate admitted Work is therefore an **Autonomous Research Director Pass**.
